# Brag Plan: Killa Nikkei

## What is this app?
An upscale Peruvian-Japanese Nikkei restaurant in Salt Lake City. The site is a cinematic, reservation-focused experience that treats dining as ceremony, with gold-on-black visual identity and moon imagery.

## The angle
This is not a burger joint. It is a ritual. The video should feel like entering a temple of flavor, where precision and restraint create an atmosphere of calm intention. The moon motif and Nikkei heritage are the visual soul.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The tagline that defines everything: "A Flavor Ritual made Under the Moon." Gold text emerging from darkness, like moonlight.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The hero section with the Killa logo and atmospheric video/imagery
- "Two Worlds, One Moon" headline with the three pillars: Nikkei Heritage, Intentional Craft, Atmosphere of Calm
- Signature dishes: Ceviche Killa, Killa Old Fashioned, Crab Causa with their poetic descriptions
- The reservation CTA with "Limited seating. Intentional dining."

## Outro / punchline
"Reserve Your Table" call to action with address and the tagline return: "A Flavor Ritual made Under the Moon."

## User flow worth showing
Landing page only site focused on reservations. The flow is: atmosphere → philosophy → dishes → reserve. No app interaction to simulate.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: upscale restaurant film, temple of flavor aesthetic
- Interpretation: Dramatic, slow reveals. Gold on black. Let each moment land with weight. Big type, restrained motion. The restraint IS the luxury.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0a0a0a (near black)
- Accent: #D4AF37 (gold/brass)
- Text: #FFFFFF (white) and gold
- Display font: Serif (elegant, menu-grade)
- Body font: Sans-serif (clean, modern)
- Strongest visual element: The moon motif, gold typography on black, the atmospheric hero

## Share copy (draft)
Two worlds. One moon. Killa brings Peruvian warmth and Japanese precision to Salt Lake City. Reserve your ritual.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 (steady and clean, fits polished/cinematic)
- Music treatment: Start at 0s, low volume (0.25-0.30), gentle fade out at end
- Music cue guidance: Vol-12 preset exists. Use strong cues for major reveals (hook landing, dish reveal, outro). Beat grid for any sequential elements.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; gold glow elements breathe slightly with bass
- SFX posture: sparse; 2-3 elegant cues only
- Audio-coupled moments: hook text reveal (soft bell), dish names appearing (subtle drop), outro logo (resonant bell)
- Restraint rule: no rapid-fire sounds, no aggressive impacts. This is a temple.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3s
Gold text "A Flavor Ritual" types or fades elegantly on black. Then "made Under the Moon." appears below.
Sequential/interaction: text reveal, not simultaneous
Audio intent: mysterious, inviting warmth
Audio-coupled idea: soft bell or resonant tone as final word lands
Music: low cinematic bed begins
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Philosophy — 4s
"Two Worlds, One Moon" headline. The three pillars appear: Nikkei Heritage, Intentional Craft, Atmosphere of Calm. Gold accents on dark.
Sequential/interaction: three pillars appear one by one
Audio intent: building presence
Audio-coupled idea: subtle drop sound for each pillar
Transition mood: dramatic → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Dishes — 6s
Signature dishes showcase. "Ceviche Killa" with ingredients. "Killa Old Fashioned" with ingredients. Perhaps a food image or the gold typography treatment.
Sequential/interaction: dish cards or names appear sequentially
Audio intent: sophisticated, appetizing
Audio-coupled idea: card sounds for dish reveals
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Reserve — 4s
"Reserve Your Table" CTA. "Limited seating. Intentional dining." Address: 279 E 300 S, Salt Lake City.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: warm invitation
Audio-coupled idea: none or subtle presence
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 3s
Killa logo or wordmark. Return of tagline "A Flavor Ritual made Under the Moon."
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: memorable close, bell tone
Audio-coupled idea: resonant bell on logo landing
Music: fade out under final beat
