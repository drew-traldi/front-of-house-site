# Killa Nikkei Brag Plan

## Project Summary
- **Site**: https://www.killanikkei.com/
- **Type**: Upscale Peruvian-Japanese Nikkei restaurant
- **Location**: 279 E 300 S, Salt Lake City, UT
- **Brand**: Gold (#D4AF37) on black, elegant, cinematic

## Brag Configuration
- **Tone**: cinematic
- **Format**: landscape (1920×1080)
- **Duration**: 22 seconds
- **Music**: vol-12 (upbeat, sophisticated)
- **Voice**: off

## Hook
**"A Flavor Ritual made Under the Moon."**

## Creative Angle
Showcase the site as a cinematic experience: the fire-pit patio atmosphere, the elegant UI, and signature dishes. Every frame shows real site content or real food photography from the restaurant.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1: Hook (0–4s)
- **Background**: Fire-pit patio hero photo (Ken Burns slow zoom out from 1.1× to 1.0×)
- **Overlay**: Hook text fades in with gold accent
- **Audio**: Music begins, impact bell at 2.5s
- **Copy**: 
  - "A Flavor Ritual" (gold, 96px)
  - "made Under the Moon." (white italic, 48px)

### Scene 2: Browser Scroll (4–10s)
- **Visual**: Browser frame showing actual killanikkei.com homepage
- **Motion**: Smooth scroll through the full page (hero → menu → about → gallery)
- **Audio**: Continuing music
- **Purpose**: Show the real site UI and content

### Scene 3: Dish Showcase (10–16s)
- **Background**: Site menu section screenshot with Ken Burns
- **Overlay**: Three dish cards with real food photos
  - Ceviche Killa (with actual ceviche photo)
  - Killa Old Fashioned (with actual cocktail photo)
  - Crab Causa (with actual causa photo)
- **Motion**: Cards reveal sequentially with lift animation
- **Audio**: Drop SFX at 11s on first card

### Scene 4: Reserve CTA (16–19s)
- **Background**: Site reservation section (Ken Burns pan)
- **Overlay**: "Reserve Your Table" CTA with address
- **Motion**: Text fades in over site content

### Scene 5: Logo Outro (19–22s)
- **Background**: Patio photo (dimmed)
- **Overlay**: KILLA wordmark + tagline "Two Worlds, One Moon."
- **Motion**: Scale-in with slight bounce
- **Audio**: Music fade

## Technical Notes
- All backgrounds use real screenshots from the live site
- Food photos downloaded from actual site imagery
- Browser scroll shows full page capture at 1920px wide
- At least 70% of screen time shows real site visuals
- Text overlays use scrim for legibility
